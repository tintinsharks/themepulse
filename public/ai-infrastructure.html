Custom silicon + merchant GPUs. Hyperscalers route compute to in-house ASICs (Trainium / TPU / MTIA / Maia). AI
Connectivity
⏱ MID
Chip-to-chip interconnect IC bench. Pure-play silicon vendors moving bits between AI chips: ALAB (PCIe/CXL), CRDO (SerDes/AECs), MRVL (optical DSPs), AAOI/MXL (optical components). ★ bottleneck Every GPU/ASIC needs an interconnect IC — captures TAM regardless of which compute vendor wins. ⚠ thesis killer CPO compresses pluggable TAM faster than expected. ALAB insulated (PCIe/CXL is electrical-only, CPO-orthogonal).

Networking
+ Components
⏱ MID
Box-level networking + physical supply chain. Switches (ANET, CSCO), coherent line systems (CIEN), ODMs/contract manufacturers (CLS, JBL), high-density connectors + passives (APH, TEL). The physical chassis around the interconnect ICs. ★ bottleneck APH/TEL high-density backplane connectors have multi-quarter lead times. CLS/JBL ODM capacity is hyperscaler-allocated. ⚠ thesis killer Hyperscalers in-house networking builds (cuts ANET/CSCO share) OR connector commoditization compresses APH/TEL margins.

Semicap
+ Materials
⏱ MID
Selective: test/inspection > deposition. SNDK CapEx only 4% of revenue → memory profits accrue to producers, not equipment. Indium upstream is byproduct-coupled to zinc. ★ bottleneck ASML EUV monopoly gates leading-edge logic. Indium concentration in China = systemic risk. ⚠ thesis killer NAND CapEx surprise to upside reverses the "test > deposition" thesis.
Lead-lag horizon. Demand commitments (Neoclouds + Hyperscalers) flow downstream over 1-4 quarters per layer. A hyperscaler capex print today benefits foundry in ~1Q, memory in ~1-2Q, photonics test in ~2-3Q, DC+cooling in ~1-3Q, power in ~2-8Q (longest cycle).
Time-phase rule. ⏱ EARLY layers have most upside left (Power, Photonics). LATE layers price in much of the thesis (Compute Silicon, near-peak Memory GM). Use phase as position-sizing input — overweight EARLY, lighten LATE.
Bottleneck rule. Bottlenecks live where capacity is least fungible (test, packaging, advanced packaging, hermetic seal, permitting, thermal density) — NOT in raw materials. Margin accrues at the bottleneck.

5 · AI Connectivity — Interconnect Bench "Get bits between chips" — distinct from chips themselves
Names whose role is moving bits between AI chips and clusters. Sits between Custom Silicon (compute) and Photonics (deeper supply chain). Captures value from EVERY GPU/ASIC scale-out — independent of which silicon vendor wins.
⏱ MID
800G live · 1.6T qualifying · CPO 2027-28 · CXL/PCIe Gen6 ramping
Electrical interconnect (in-rack / chip-to-chip)
ALAB CRDO MRVL AVGO
ALAB = PCIe/CXL retimers solving GPU comms · CRDO = SerDes + AECs (Active Electrical Cables) · MRVL = optical DSPs + custom SerDes · AVGO = switch silicon + custom interconnect
★ EVERY GPU/ASIC NEEDS ONE — captures TAM regardless of which compute vendor wins
Optical interconnect (rack-to-rack / row-to-row)
AAOI MXL CIEN
AAOI = 800G/1.6T pluggable specialist · MXL = optical DSP pivot from broadband · CIEN = coherent line systems (long-haul)
★ 800G→1.6T CYCLE NOW · CPO TRANSITION 2027-28 = re-rating event for survivors
Thesis killer: co-packaged optics (CPO) compresses pluggable TAM faster than expected. AAOI most exposed; MRVL/CRDO have CPO roadmaps that absorb the transition. ALAB is insulated (PCIe/CXL is electrical-only and CPO-orthogonal).

★ Compute bottleneck
Advanced packaging (TSMC CoWoS) is THE choke for GPU/ASIC volumes. ASML EUV monopoly gates leading-edge logic.
★ Memory bottleneck
HBM probe-card test (FORM) and HBM advanced-packaging are the gates. Supply pre-booked through 2027.
★ Connectivity advantage
ALAB/CRDO/MRVL/AAOI/MXL get paid on EVERY GPU/ASIC scale-out — agnostic to which compute vendor wins. PCIe/CXL (ALAB) is CPO-orthogonal so insulated from optical transition risk.
★ Photonics bottleneck
TEST + ALIGNMENT + HERMETIC PACKAGING — not raw materials. AEHR/CAMT/ONTO/FN capture margin during demand surges.
★ DC + Cooling bottleneck
Power-secured sites (DLR/EQIX) command pricing power. Liquid cooling (VRT/MOD) required >50kW/rack — air-cooling caps out. EME/FIX MEP backlog through 2027.
★ Power bottleneck
Permitting + interconnect queue is multi-year structural. Grid-equipment + construction (GEV/ETN/PWR/MYRG) front-runs IPPs.
⚠ WFE caveat
Memory earnings expand WITHOUT a NAND CapEx boom. SNDK at 4% capex/revenue. Avoid extrapolating high GM into greenfield orders.
⚠ CPO transition risk
CPO doesn't shrink the supply chain — it relocates bottlenecks. Pluggable specialists (AAOI) face TAM compression. FN/AEHR absorb new work.

Lead-lag horizon: Demand commitments (top) → flow downstream over 1-4 quarters per layer. A hyperscaler capex print today benefits foundry in ~1Q, memory in ~1-2Q, photonics test in ~2-3Q, datacenter+cooling in ~1-3Q (depends on permit status), power in ~2-8Q (longest cycle).
Time-phase rule: ⏱ EARLY layers have the most upside left (e.g., Power gen). LATE layers are pricing in much of the thesis already (e.g., custom silicon, near-peak memory GM). Use phase as a position-sizing input — overweight EARLY, lighten LATE.
Bottleneck rule: bottlenecks (★) live where capacity is least fungible (test, packaging, advanced packaging, hermetic seal, permitting, thermal density). NOT in raw materials. Margin accrues at the bottleneck.
Failure-mode rule: every layer's thesis killer is annotated inline. Position size proportional to confidence × distance from kill condition.
